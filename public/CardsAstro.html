ASTRONOMY

Astronomy is known to many as the science of space. Many are familliar to the concepts of stars, planets, moons, and even aliens. However Astronomy is more about the Universe's life and how we got here. The early humans built a solid foundation for astronomy. They discovered concepts such as stars, constellations, moons, and even planets. But all paled in advancing science compared to the telescope. The telescope allowed us to take a closer look at objects we couldn't see before. New moons, new planets, even new things like galaxies, further expanding our understanding of the universe. Nowadays, with the most powerful telescopes, we focus on piecing together the history of our world. We send and explore satellites to uncover what we couldn't see before. Being an Astronomer doesn't mean you have to become an astronaut, you can help in the ground as well. Building and maintaining essential satellites that keep the world going and our understanding increasing faster.
