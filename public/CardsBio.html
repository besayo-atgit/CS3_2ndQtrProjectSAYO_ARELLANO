BIOLOGY

Biology is the study of living things, and so is the one most people encounter. Every bite of food, every scroll on a phone, every jog, biology happens. Understanding biology is understanding ourselves and our strenghts and weaknesses. It offers a close look at all living things, and can create parallels between humans and other organisms. Biology helps us understand more about ourselves, and can be used to help us. For example, the vaccine, a life saving invention, takes advantage of our immune system to build resistance against deadly pathogens. It is also useful for hospitals worldwide, treating ilnesses, healing injuries, or even doing complex surgery that would've been difficult without modern day tools.
