CHEMISTRY

Chemistry is the study of the reactions matter makes with other matter. It is a vital part of understanding the world, and it even helps us live. Chemistry dives deep into how atoms act, and how the acts affect other atoms. Chemical reactions happen everywhere, from the light turning on, food being cooked then eaten, and even fire burning. Chemists help create or neutralize chemicals that act and affect us differently. They can find a chemical that reacts in just the right way to help us beneficially, or find and prevent the spread and use of toxic substances.
