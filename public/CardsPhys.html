PHYSICS

Physics is a topic heavy on math. However, because of this, it is near fundamental to the universe. Everything from this computer to your body not destroying itself is reliant on the laws of physics. But what is physics? In short, it is all about matter and its behaviors. It dictates how fast a thing is, how much force a thing exerts, how fast the change in speed is, all of which affect a lot more of the world than many think. Physics is what explains gravity, it explains how far a ball thrown will reach. And because it explains it, we can use it to predict things. Physics has many equations that can fit your needs, even if you're missing some things. Because of this, Physicists are well respected among most fields. Their work spans many fields, from the common phemomena to both subatomic and universal effects.
